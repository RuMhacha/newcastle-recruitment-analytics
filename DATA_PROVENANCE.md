# Data Provenance and Public Repository Scope

This public repository contains the analytical code, methodology, QA framework,
frozen decision-support outputs, and selected derived recruitment artefacts for
the Newcastle United Recruitment Analytics project.

## Source data

The original football source datasets used during development are intentionally
not redistributed in this public repository.

Development data included tabular football statistics obtained from external
football-data sources, including FBref/Sports Reference-derived tables. Those
source datasets and closely source-like processed tables are retained only in
the private analytical archive.

Users wishing to reproduce the full pipeline should obtain equivalent source
data independently and ensure that their use complies with the applicable
provider terms, licensing conditions, and attribution requirements.

## Published data

CSV files included in this public repository are selected derived analytical
outputs produced by the project pipeline. They are intended to demonstrate the
decision-support architecture, scoring logic, validation framework, succession
analysis, and executive reporting workflow.

They should not be interpreted as an independently licensed replacement for
the underlying source football datasets.

## Human decision safeguard

The system is designed as decision support only. Statistical outputs do not
constitute final recruitment approval. Final recruitment decisions remain
subject to validated human scouting and football-domain assessment.
